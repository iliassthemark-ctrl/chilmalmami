CHILLMALMAMI
RECORDS

CHILLMALMAMI Records is our Afro House label, born straight out of the energy of our parties. What started on the dancefloor in Cologne has grown into a sound that blends African roots with deep house energy — built for moving, feeling, and connecting. We collaborate with some of the most exciting artists in the Afro House scene. From hypnotic Amapiano grooves to spiritual Tribal House, every release carries the CHILLMALMAMI DNA.

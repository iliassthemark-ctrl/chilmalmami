About us

CHILL MAL MAMI

Born from a love of deep grooves and African rhythms, CHILLMALMAMI has become one of the most exciting Afro House event brands in Germany. What started as an underground movement has grown into a full-blown cultural phenomenon.

Our mission is simple — bring people together through the power of Afro House music. From the hypnotic beats of Amapiano to the spiritual depths of Tribal House, every night with CHILLMALMAMI is an experience you won't forget.

About us

CHILL MAL MAMI

A room full of rhythm, energy, and people who came for the same reason: to feel good.

We created a place where you don’t have to play a role, impress anyone, or be anything other than yourself.

Come with your friends.
Come with someone you love.
Or come alone and see who you meet along the way.

Dance in the middle of the crowd. Close your eyes. Lose yourself in the music. Laugh too loud. Stay longer than you planned.

Maybe you flirt. Maybe you don’t.
Maybe you dance with someone.
Maybe the only thing you fall in love with tonight is the music.

No pressure. No expectations. Just good music, good energy, and a space where everyone is welcome.

Whatever the night brings, let it feel right.
There’s a place for you here.
